      Privacy, and what this cannot tell you

      What stays private

      • Your password is read from the field and analyzed by code running in your browser. Typing sends no requests.
      • There are no cookies, no analytics, and nothing written to local storage. The field and any generated password are wiped when you leave the page.
      • When the page opens it loads its fonts and one open-source library, zxcvbn. That is the only network traffic, and you can confirm it in your browser's Network tab while you type.

      What it cannot tell you

      • It does not look your password up in breach databases, because that would mean sending something derived from it. Use your password manager's breach alerts for that.
      • A score is an estimate based on common attack patterns. Someone targeting you personally may do better, and reusing a password across sites matters more than any score.
      • If you are unsure about any page, including this one, test a password with the same structure instead of the real one.
      Strength estimates come from zxcvbn, an open-source library from Dropbox. Estimates, not guarantees.
